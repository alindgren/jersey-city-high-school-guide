# Repository agent policy

This repository publishes admissions guidance used by Jersey City families. Accuracy and traceability take priority over speed.

## Scope

- Cover Jersey City public schools, Jersey City public charter schools, Hudson County Schools of Technology options available to Jersey City residents, and Catholic high schools in Jersey City.
- Do not add Hoboken schools or non-Catholic private schools unless the owner changes the scope.

## Source rules

- Treat school, district, county, College Board, New Jersey Department of Education, and other first-party government pages as authoritative.
- An unofficial article or social post may be a lead, but it cannot support a published factual change by itself.
- Never convert a prior-cycle date into a current deadline. Label unconfirmed timing as expected or as a planning reference.
- Preserve direct source links and record the date each source was checked.
- Do not quote more source text than necessary. Summarize facts in original language.

## Change rules

- Keep each pull request narrowly focused on verified changes.
- Explain the old fact, the new fact, and the primary-source evidence in the pull request.
- Do not change rankings or introduce sponsored placement.
- Do not merge or deploy automatically. The repository owner reviews and merges every admissions update.
- Run `npm run build` and `npm run check:cloudflare` before opening a pull request.
